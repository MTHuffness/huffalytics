The loop

Play-by-play in. A graded read out. Every week.

One pipeline runs from the snap data to the plan to the report, and the report feeds the next plan. Nothing is a one-off opinion.

  1. 01Play-by-playEvery snap of every game from the open nflverse data, plus charting: box counts, blitzes, run lanes, pressure.
  2. 02RatingsExpected points added per play for each offense and defense, adjusted for the opponents faced and blended with a preseason prior.
  3. 03ProjectionA full box score for every game: team points anchored on the market, then every player's line from his share of the offense.
  4. 04The planAn AI-written game plan from a dossier of the numbers: the script, what swings it, and the overs and unders.
  5. 05The gameKickoff. Every call is graded off the box score the moment it lands.
  6. 06The reportAn AI-written post-game report: what decided it, the turning points, each team's blueprint, and a review of the plan that feeds the next one.

Overs and unders

Every call, graded three ways

A call is one player, one market, one side of a line: over 64.5 rushing yards, under 5.5 receptions, to score a touchdown. The plan has to say why. A volume call rides the script, an efficiency call rides the matchup, and the plan labels which is which.

From the 2026 season the calls are read against Kalshi, the regulated prediction exchange, where each player market is a ladder of rungs. Every call is graded at three cushions down that ladder, and the record at each is published on the week board and on every game page.

  • Normal is the line the plan called.
  • Edge is the Kalshi rung nearest halfway between that line and the easy end of the ladder.
  • Edge+ is the last rung: the lowest N+ on an over, the highest under-N on an under.

Each rung shows Kalshi's last pregame price, so the record reads as what those tickets would have cost. Huffalytics is not affiliated with Kalshi, and nothing here is betting advice.

Streaming targets

Kickers and defenses are ranked by models fit on 2013 through 2025: implied team points carry most of a kicker's week, and a low opponent implied total plus being the favorite carries most of a defense's. Quarterbacks, backs, receivers and tight ends outside the starter line are ranked by the game projection. The top five in each tab are the week's stream picks, and a pick is a hit when it finishes in the top ten.
