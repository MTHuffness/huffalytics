The read

Green Bay by a field goal, with the scoring split doing the work.

more

The Packers have moved the ball all season between the 20s (+0.029 field EPA, positive in four of five weeks) and stopped at the 20 — 50% red-zone touchdown rate, "red zone stalls" tagged twice, 1-of-4 in Detroit. Carolina is the one defense that hands those trips back: 78.6% red-zone touchdown rate allowed (30th), +0.341 EPA/play inside its own 20, and it now plays without Jaycee Horn, Mike Jackson and Akayleb Evans (ruled out Wednesday) at corner and without Jaelan Phillips off the edge. The same unit is 32nd in yards per carry (5.16) and 32nd in rush yards allowed, so Green Bay's dead run game (2.91 YPC) gets its one usable week. Carolina goes the other way. It needs 68-76 snaps and a clean pocket; it gets a defense ranked 5th in EPA allowed between the 20s, 5th in yards per attempt and 9th in pass yards per game, and it plays this one with Taylor Moton on IR for the year, Damien Lewis and Torricelli Simpkins ruled out. Week 7 is the template: five sacks, 27 attempts, 163 yards, 56 plays.

Why

The mechanism chain is short.

more

Carolina cannot pressure without blitzing and cannot cover with its top three corners out, so Love gets time and the end zone; Green Bay leads, and leading is the only state in which this offense runs. Carolina trailing means fewer Hubbard carries (seven in week 7's chase) and more dropbacks against a front-seven-plus-safety group that has taken the ball away in each of its last three games. No weather flag — 50 degrees, light wind.

What would change it

Matthew Golden's shoulder (questionable; Rotowire and FantasyPros both filed Thursday that he is expected to play).

more

Keisean Nixon's late shoulder designation — if he sits, Carolina's receivers get the matchup back. And Green Bay's own interior: Rhyan, Monk and Glover are all out, so an early Panthers pressure wave could flip the script to the one Carolina needs.
