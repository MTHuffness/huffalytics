Seattle squeezes the field and folds at the goal line. Walker gets in.

Two defenses that make you earn every yard between the 20s — the difference is Seattle concedes touchdowns on the few trips it allows while Kansas City gives up field goals.

The read

This is a low-total game between the best field defense in the league and one of the best goal-line defenses, and they are not the same team.

more

Seattle has allowed the fewest yards per attempt and the least EPA per play between the 20s, but opponents have scored touchdowns on better than four of five red-zone trips — 7-for-7 across weeks 4 through 6. Kansas City is the mirror image: 24th-ish between the 20s by rank but third in red-zone touchdown rate allowed, with three opponent trips in week 6 producing zero touchdowns and two kicks. So the shape is: few drives, hard yards, and Seattle's points arriving as threes while Kansas City's arrive as sevens.

Why

Kansas City's whole engine is Kenneth Walker and a clean pocket — league-low pressure allowed, 5.5 yards a carry, three-to-six explosive runs in every game.

more

Seattle takes the run away between the 20s (first in rush EPA allowed) and generates the third-most pressure in the league against a Chiefs line now without Jaylon Moore (concussion, ruled out Sunday per Rotowire) on top of Josh Simmons on IR, with Rice and Thornton gone from the target tree. That caps Mahomes' yardage and KC's trip count — but it does not help Seattle once Kansas City reaches the 20, which is where Walker has scored six times in five games and where Seattle's one real weakness lives. Going the other way, Seattle cannot run (negative rush EPA in all six games) into a Chiefs front allowing 3.5 a carry, so Darnold throws, Jaxon Smith-Njigba eats his usual 40-plus percent of the air yards, and the drives die around the 20 against a bend-don't-break defense that has conceded one red-zone touchdown in three trips twice already. Three giveaways in each of weeks 3, 5 and 6 are the tail on that.

What would change it

Kansas City scoring first and leading: then Walker's carries climb, Seattle throws 45 times and the whole Chiefs passing-under case thins.

more

A Walker explosive run — his distribution has a fat right tail no front has closed — also blows up the yardage reads. And every price here is the exchange's, with thin volume behind it.
