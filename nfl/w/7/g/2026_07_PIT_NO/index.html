Warren runs at the league's softest front. Shough throws with a broken hand.

Pittsburgh should lead and lean on Jaylen Warren against a New Orleans front that gets run through; the Saints bend at the field but hold at the goal line, so the points may come in threes.

The read

Pittsburgh is a field-goal favorite in a 44.5-point dome game and has the one clean matchup on the card: New Orleans allows 4.79 yards a carry (28th), 138 rushing yards a game (30th), ranks 31st in rush success allowed and dead last in fantasy points to backs.

more

Pittsburgh's own offense has no vertical element left — 4.1 air yards per throw in week 6, zero explosive passes, 152 yards after the catch — so if the Steelers get in front, the game turns into Warren carries, short throws to Metcalf and a field-goal unit. New Orleans is the mirror image: a dead ground game (3.79 YPC), a quarterback playing through two broken bones in his non-throwing hand who has averaged 27.5 attempts over the last two weeks, and a red-zone offense that finishes 76.5% of trips. Pittsburgh's defense ranks 10th in pass EPA allowed, 4th in points allowed to quarterbacks and 8th in red-zone touchdown rate allowed.

Why

Both defenses give up the field and harden at the 20: New Orleans ranks 28th between the 20s but 7th in red-zone EPA allowed and 11th in trips-to-touchdowns; Pittsburgh is 14th and 8th.

more

That is the yards-without-touchdowns profile on both sides — yardage calls over touchdown calls, and a live case for Boswell, who has made two or more in four of six games. Script-wise, our week-6 review learned that Pittsburgh only throws 45-plus times when it is chasing; as the favorite against a team with no run game to hold the ball, the carries should finally arrive. New Orleans ran 38 times in week 6 only because it led — trailing, Miller's workload evaporates and the backfield splits three ways.

What would change it

An early Saints lead inverts everything: Shough's attempts climb, Olave's volume returns and Warren's carries die, exactly as happened in weeks 4 and 6.

more

Cesar Ruiz's knee and Nathan Shepherd's illness are unresolved; Kaden Elliss and Anfernee Jennings returning makes the New Orleans front healthier than the one Atlanta gashed in week 4. Rico Dowdle's activation would trim Warren's carry floor.
